# Chapter 2 - Tension

Anakin could only feel a little sigh escape his lips a he stared at Ahsoka in front of him. Somewhere inside of him he was really glad to see her again. For her to be this close again. For her to worry about him and care as she once had. But another, even bigger part - which probably was directly fulled by all the alcohol he had consumed this day - felt cornered by her. Trapped under her caring gaze. What was she even after? What was it that she tried to accomplish here? Save him? What a joke. The Anakin she and his old master want to protect is gone. He has been replaced by whatever husk of a man was left behind in this stinking chair filling himself up with whatever had a percentage warning on the label. No one could save him. He couldn't be saved. He did not deserve to be saved.

“Ahsoka come on… you know full and well that you are better off without me. You severed all the ties to your old life for a reason after all. You cannot save me… you cannot save the order. Hell, I cannot even save myself, there is nothing left to save. I fulfilled my purpose and now I wait till the force decides that my time has come to die and go over into the living force… or whatever Yoda taught about this mumbo jumbo… Just go, live your life… be happy… just leave me behind.” 

“Not happening! … I left you once, that’s a mistake I will not repeat again.”

Hearing these words almost made Anakin fall down in tears again. She won't leave him again? He really wished that was true, but he knew better. Even if she did, he would fuck it up again. Just like he had before. He could not save her from the orders persecution. He couldn't be the one she needed. Hell, he couldn't even be the husband Padmé deserved or wanted. He is a fuck up, that is all he is and ever will be, there is no need for someone else to fall into the pit of fire that was Anakin Skywalker.

"Anakin?" He could hear her voice echo in his mind. He jerked his head upwards to look into the concerned eyes of one Ahsoka Tano. He couldn't bear looking at her.

"D-Don't look at me like that..." he huffed.

"Like what?" She challenged.

"Lik- Like you care! Like you would stay! Like you... you pity me..." He spat back, but lost all his bravado at the end. He had no energy for this. He wanted to slowly disappear not to engage in meaningless banter with his old Padawan. He couldn't be saved or healed, does she not see that?

"Is that what this is about?" Ahsoka said while gesturing at him grandly, "Does the great Anakin Skywalker not want to be pitied? Does he not want to be cared for by people who want nothing more than the best for him?" She asked, her voice getting a bit more heated. Why was she getting angry now? What was the point? 

"It's not about what I want or don't want Ahsoka... It's about being realistic." He huffed, not enough energy there to put some force behind those words. Maybe he didn't even believe them himself. "Look at me. Do you see someone who can be healed? Who can be saved? What's the point Ahsoka..."

"What do you mean it's not about what you want?! Of course it is! All you want is to huddle up here in this disgusting bin of a room and drink yourself to death! That has nothing to do with whether you can be helped or not or if I care about you or not. But it has everything to do with you not WANTING help!" Ahsoka retorted back jumping up from her chair in the process walking around the room. If her heated words were not directed at him he might have thought this to be quite the cute display.

"I can't be helped." He answered even before he really thought about those words and decided to say them. They came just naturally off his lips as he locked at her as if what he said was the most sacred and true thing he ever said. He could even see her halt at that. Halt at his eyes that seemed so sure about this, so sure of it being the only truth there was.

"Why?" Ahsoka asked "Why can't you be helped? Do you even know yourself?" She asked exasperated and he could see the hurt in her eyes and feel it over their bond. He didn't even need to probe the neglected and forgotten bond of them to feel it, it was broadcasted over the force so clearly, that every force sensitive in the vicinity would feel it.

Why was he so sure? For a moment he thought of her question as he could feel his eyes water again at her hurt. His chest began to shrink and feel numb as he could feel his mechanical hand jaggedly fall closed as he apparently clenched it. His breathing got ragged and he quickly took a big swig of his alcohol, only to choke on it and cough it all right up again. Ahsoka made no move to do anything about that still staring at him. Waiting for an answer. He looked away from her and said what came to his mind, what felt like the truth.

"Because I do not deserve it." he almost whispered the words as he felt the tension in his body fall again and himself sink deeper into the hard uncomfortable chair, his back already hurt from it, but he couldn't care less.
